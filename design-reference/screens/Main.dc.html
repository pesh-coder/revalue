Rules

  • Never pure white as a page background. Never pure black as text.
  • Forest is reserved for buttons, links and focus. Deep carries large dark bands and headings.
  • Clay is only a mark: the tag dot, a short rule. Never on buttons, large fills or body text.
  • Text on a photograph sits on a deep overlay of at least 60%, and passes AA at every point it covers.
  • On a deep band the button flips to surface fill with deep words, 11.03:1.
