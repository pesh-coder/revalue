Smart segregation prototype

Status
Testing, as of 30 October 2026
Origin
Started as a final-year project at Kyambogo University.

1Overview

A prototype bin that uses a camera and sensors to sort waste into separate compartments.

2Problem

Mixed waste loses value. We wanted to test whether sorting at the bin could keep materials separate with less effort.

3Approach

A camera and classifier on a Raspberry Pi work with an Arduino, which reads the sensors and turns a sorting disc.

4Work completed

Built a working prototype. It sorts into three compartments: metal, organic and plastic. Software logs each sorting event to a small dashboard.
