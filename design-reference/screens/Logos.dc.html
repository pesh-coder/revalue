Logo direction · The value loop

Three candidates for a mark and wordmark

Each mark is one colour, built from one or two simple forms, and has to read on its own on a bin, a social avatar and a favicon. The wordmark is Newsreader 600, tracked -0.02em. Shown at 128, 32 and 16px, then as horizontal, stacked and mono lock-ups.

Candidate A

Path R

One unbroken line draws the R: up the stem, round the bowl, out along the leg. Material that stays on one route instead of breaking off.

Main risk

Single-letter R marks are crowded, in logistics, property and fintech especially. The least distinctive of the three; it leans on the wordmark to be recognised.

Value loop

An open ring, and a single dot leaving through its gap. Material comes round once more and leaves as something useful. Two forms, no letters, no arrowhead.

Main risk

Open rings sit close to power, refresh and loading icons, and to the circular-economy loops many recyclers use. The offset dot keeps it apart. Never add an arrowhead or a second dot.

Candidate C

Return

A line goes down, turns, and climbs higher than where it started: discarded on the left, useful on the right. In section it also reads as the profile of a bin.

Main risk

Close to U-shaped monograms, U-turn signage and magnet icons. Below 24px the uneven arms can flatten into a plain letter U.
