Waste Discovery Review

Status: In developmentSolutionsUpdated 30 October 2026

Who it is for: hotels, schools, offices and other sites in Greater Kampala that want a clear picture of their waste.

What we would do

  • Walk the process with your team.
  • Identify the main waste streams.
  • Observe separation and contamination.
  • Record collection arrangements and costs where available.
  • Give you a short action plan.

What we will not do

We will not guarantee savings or recovery before we have measured anything. If a recovery partner is not confirmed, we will say so.

What affects the cost

Site size, number of waste streams, depth of assessment, travel and reporting needs. How pricing works
