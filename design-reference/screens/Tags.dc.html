Key component

Status tag

A small, quiet pill: a 6px clay mark and the words in clay-text on clay-tint. The words carry the meaning, so it never relies on colour. Screen readers hear "Status:" before the value.

Anatomy: 24px minimum height, 3px 10px 3px 8px padding, 6px gap, label-sm 12px 600 at 0.04em, fully rounded.
